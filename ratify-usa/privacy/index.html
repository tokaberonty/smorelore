Privacy Policy

Ratify USA Constitution Test
Last updated: November 12, 2026

The short version

This app collects nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third party is sent anything about you or your child. Every bit of progress stays on the phone it was made on.

What the app stores, and where

The app keeps three things on the device:

  • Progress. Which states are signed, stars and high scores, saved waves, which flashcard chests are claimed, which questions were answered right or wrong, practice test scores, the study streak and aura.
  • Settings. Whether sound effects are on.
  • The unlock. Whether the one-time purchase has been made.

All three live in the app's own storage on the device. They are never uploaded, never backed up to us, and never shared. We cannot see them, and neither can anyone else.

Personal information

The app asks for no name, no email address, no date of birth, no photographs and no location. It has no text field anywhere, so there is nothing to type a name into. It never uses the microphone or the camera.

Children

This app is intended for middle school students studying the U.S. Constitution, and is used by children. No personal information is collected from anyone, of any age. Because nothing is collected, nothing is used, disclosed, sold or retained.

Network

The app makes no network connections.

The one exception is a purchase. If you buy the unlock, the purchase runs through Apple's App Store, the same as any other in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment and tells the app whether the purchase succeeded. We receive no payment details, no Apple Account information and nothing about your child. Apple's own privacy policy covers that transaction.

Deleting the data

Delete the app. Everything the app stored goes with it. A purchase comes back afterwards with Restore purchase.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
