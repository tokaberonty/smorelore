Restore a purchase

Settings (the quill at the top right), then Unlock or restore, then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

No sound

Check the phone's silent mode. The app follows it. Sound effects can also be turned off in Settings.

A state is locked

States open in order. Sign a state to open the next one. The first 3 are free, and the unlock opens the rest.

Start the flashcards over

In Flashcards, tap the three dots at the top right, then Start over.

Move to a new phone

Progress lives only on the phone it was made on. A purchase comes back with Restore purchase.
