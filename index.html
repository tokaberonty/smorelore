smorelore

a campfire, a craft, a story after dark

a campfire,
embers crackle
and lift off the flames
they melt into the night sky

the joy of simple things

wisdom lives in stories,
which drift off like smoke
lore is where they settle

back to the craft,
the mind refreshed and focused
and filled with fire

build with care
keep it simple
leave this place better
than you found it

pull up a log

smorelore · first sketched october 2026
