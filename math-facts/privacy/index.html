Privacy Policy

Math Facts Practice Test
Last updated: October 13, 2026

The short version

This app collects nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third party is sent anything about you or your child. Every practice record stays on the phone it was made on.

What the app stores, and where

The app keeps four things on the device:

  • The practice record. Which facts were answered, whether they were right, and how long they took.
  • Settings. Speech, pacing, problem layout, and the theme.
  • Decks you build or keep.
  • Badges earned.

All four live in the app's own storage on the device. They are never uploaded, never backed up to us, and never shared. We cannot see them, and neither can anyone else.

Personal information

The app asks for no name, no email address, no date of birth, no photographs and no location. It has no text field that asks who the child is. A deck name is the only text anyone types, it stays on the device with everything else, and nothing requires it to be a real name.

Children

This app is intended for elementary school practice and is used by children. No personal information is collected from anyone, of any age. Because nothing is collected, nothing is used, disclosed, sold or retained.

Network

The app makes no network connections.

The one exception is a purchase. If you buy the unlock, the purchase runs through Apple's App Store, the same as any other in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment and tells the app whether the purchase succeeded. We receive no payment details, no Apple Account information and nothing about your child. Apple's own privacy policy covers that transaction.

Printing

Worksheets, answer keys, certificates and reward charts are drawn on the device and handed to AirPrint. Nothing is uploaded to produce them, and no print service of ours exists.

Deleting the data

Delete the app. Everything the app stored goes with it.

Inside the app, Settings can clear the practice record at any time. Earned badges are kept, by design, so that deleting the app is the only way to take a sticker back.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
